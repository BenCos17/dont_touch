House-Sit Disaster Simulator

Touch objects to make a mess across multiple rooms. Before the host returns, everything must look perfect.

Mess mode: click an object to knock or shatter it.

Fix mode: click a knocked item to stand it up, or a shattered item to repair it with glue.

Inspect: double-click nearby map objects for intel and occasional glue finds.

Move: use arrow keys to walk; hit left/right room edges to pass through doors.

Use mode buttons on mobile or desktop. Survive 75 seconds with no visible damage.
